Island Amenities & Essentials

Healthcare, banking, power, and essential services for a worry-free stay.

Medical Facilities

Taniti operates one centralized hospital in Taniti City and several community clinics. The main hospital features multilingual staff speaking English, French, and Japanese.

Banking & Currency Exchange

Banks in Taniti City offer currency exchange services. While the U.S. Dollar (USD) is the standard currency, businesses also widely accept Euros, Yen, and major credit cards.

Power & Utilities

Standard electrical outlets operate at 120 volts / 60 Hz (standard Type A/B U.S. plugs). Visitors from North America do not need voltage converters.

Wi-Fi & Connectivity

Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available across resorts, hotels, and cafes in the Merriton Landing commercial district.
