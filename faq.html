Frequently Asked Questions

Essential local rules, health guidelines, and visitor travel tips.

What currency and payment methods are accepted?

The standard currency is the U.S. Dollar (USD), but euros and yen are also widely accepted. Major credit cards are supported by most restaurants and hotels.

What are the local alcohol laws?

The legal drinking age is 18. Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 AM.

What electrical outlets does Taniti use?

Outlets are standard 120 volts / 60 Hz (Type A/B), identical to those used in the United States and Canada.

Is English commonly spoken on the island?

Many younger residents and staff in Taniti City and resort hubs speak fluent English. In rural interior villages, older residents primarily speak native Tanitian.

Is Taniti safe for tourists?

Violent crime is extremely rare. However, with rising tourism, petty theft and pickpocketing can occur in busy public areas. Keep valuables secure.

Are businesses open on holidays?

Taniti observes several national cultural holidays during which non-essential businesses, local shops, and government services may close.
