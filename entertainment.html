Adventures & Entertainment

Experience outdoor excursions, natural landmarks, and Merriton Landing nightlife.

Outdoor Sightseeing

Active Volcano Tours

Hike guided trails or book helicopter flyovers for views of Taniti's active volcano crater.

Rainforest Ziplines & Hiking

Canopy tours, suspended rope walks, and guided botanical treks through tropical biodiversity.

Yellow Leaf Bay Beaches

White sand beaches, safe swimming lagoons, coral snorkeling, and chartered deep-sea fishing trips.

Merriton Landing & Culture

Nightlife & Microbrewery

Vibrant social spots featuring local island brews, seaside pubs, live musicians, and a dance club.

History & Family Fun

Visit the Taniti History Museum, check out local art galleries, or spend an evening at the retro arcade and bowling alley.

Island Bus & Boat Tours

Narrated day cruises around the perimeter and scenic island coach tours with native architectural highlights.
