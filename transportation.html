Getting to & Around Taniti

Convenient ways to explore our scenic coastlines, rainforests, and towns.

Getting to the Island

Arriving by Air

Taniti Airport accommodates small commercial jets and propeller aircraft. A regional carrier expansion is currently in progress to accommodate larger jets.

Arriving by Sea

A cruise ship docks weekly at Yellow Leaf Bay, bringing day visitors and multi-day island travelers into port.

Island Ground Transportation

Walking & Cycling

Walking & Bike Rentals

Taniti City and Merriton Landing are flat and pedestrian-friendly. Bike rental shops are scattered throughout town. Note: Helmet use is required by law.

Public & Private Transit

Buses & Shuttles

Public buses operate regularly throughout Taniti City. Private bus charters and tours serve the outer perimeter and rainforest routes.

Vehicle Rentals

Rental Cars & Taxis

Taxis are readily available at the airport and throughout Taniti City. A local car rental agency is situated directly outside the airport terminal.
